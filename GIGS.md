## 5. Cold outreach (10 per day, businesses with bad/no websites)

Find them: Google Maps → your town → any business with a broken site, a Facebook
page instead of a website, or "under construction."

**Message (DM or email):**

Hi — I was looking for a [plumber/cafe/salon] in [town] and found you. Quick note
from a local developer: your site [doesn't load properly on phones / has a broken
contact form / doesn't exist yet]. I fix and build these for flat prices ($75–$450)
and can have it done this week. Live examples of my work:
https://casperinc6-source.github.io/hire-me/ — happy to give you a free 2-minute
assessment either way. — [your name]

**Rules for outreach that actually works:**
- Be specific about THEIR site's problem — generic spam gets ignored.
- One follow-up after 3 days. Then stop. Never hound.
- Track: name, sent, replied, quoted, paid. Aim 10 sent/day → 1–2 replies → first
  paid job within 1–2 weeks is typical. First dollar often comes from someone you know —
  tell friends and family you're taking on paid work this month.
